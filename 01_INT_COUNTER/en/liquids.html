💧 Liquids

How should I pack liquids?

Basic Rules

Only liquids in containers of 100 mL or less are allowed in your carry-on baggage.

Place them in a 1 L transparent resealable plastic bag.

📌 Check These

Foods containing even a small amount of liquid are also subject to the 100 mL liquid rule.

Lip balm, hair wax, cleansing balm, toner pads, cleansing wipes, and wet wipes are also subject to the 100 mL liquid rule.

Please empty all liquids from your tumbler.

Please finish or dispose of bottled water, coffee, and other beverages before security screening.

⭐ Remember

✔ Liquid restrictions are based on the container size, not the amount of liquid inside.

✔ Containers without a capacity label may not be allowed in your carry-on baggage.

🧳 What You Can Do

For liquids over 100 mL,

✔ Use checked baggage
or
🧴 Transfer the liquid into a smaller container after purchasing an empty bottle.
