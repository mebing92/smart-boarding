Get Ready to Board ✈️

🔄 Checked → Carry-on

Items that must be removed from checked baggage and carried in your carry-on baggage.

📌 Check First

The items below cannot be transported as checked baggage.

Please remove them from your checked baggage and carry them through security screening.

🔋 Lithium Batteries

Lithium batteries are allowed in carry-on baggage only.

Restrictions may vary depending on the battery capacity and quantity.

Learn More

🚬 E-cigarettes & Lighters

E-cigarettes are allowed in carry-on baggage only.

One lighter per passenger is allowed.

⚠️ However, plasma electric lighters are prohibited in both carry-on baggage and checked baggage.

💇 Cordless Hair Iron

Depending on whether the product supports Airplane Mode or has a removable battery,

it may be allowed in carry-on baggage.

Learn More

⚡ Electric Fly Swatter

Electric fly swatters with built-in lithium batteries are allowed in carry-on baggage only.

For the most accurate information, please contact your airline.

📌 Please Note

After removing the item from your checked baggage,

Please carry it through security screening as carry-on baggage.

⏰ If your departure time is approaching, it may not be possible to check your baggage again.
