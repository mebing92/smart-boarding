SMART BOARDING ✈️

🧴 Empty Travel Bottles

Liquids over 100 mL may be carried on if transferred into containers of 100 mL or less.

📌 Before You Start

Liquids transferred into containers of 100 mL or less may be carried in your cabin baggage.

Check the airport shops where empty travel bottles are available.

⭐ Recommended Shop

Purchase your empty travel bottles at Lucky Box Gift Shop located on the same floor as the International Departure Hall.

After your purchase, you can proceed directly to the security screening checkpoint.

⭐ Lucky Box Gift Shop

Located on the 2nd Floor of the International Terminal, on the same floor as the International Departure Hall.

🕖 Opening Hours 07:00 - 21:00

Purchase your travel bottles here and head directly to the Security Checkpoint.

🏪 emart24 Convenience Store

Located on the 1st Floor of the International Terminal.

🕔 Opening Hours 05:00 - 22:00

A convenient option if you plan to visit the convenience store before departure.

💡 How to Use

① Purchase a travel bottle
↓
② Transfer your liquid into a container of 100 mL or less
↓
③ Place the container in a transparent 1 L zip-top bag
↓
④ Proceed to the Security Checkpoint

📌 Please Note

Liquid restrictions are based on the container's maximum capacity (100 mL) rather than the amount of liquid inside.

Containers without a visible capacity label may not be permitted in carry-on baggage.

For more information, please see the "Liquids" page.
