🔋 Lithium Batteries

How should I pack lithium batteries?

Lithium batteries are allowed in carry-on baggage only.

They are not allowed in checked baggage.

📌 Quantity Limits

🔋 Portable Power Banks

Up to 2 per passenger.

※ For batteries over 100 Wh, please check the capacity guidelines below.

📷 Spare Batteries for Electronic Devices

Up to 5 per passenger.

💻 Electronic Devices with Lithium Batteries

Airline approval is required if you carry more than 15 devices.

💡 Check Before Security Screening


✔ Please check whether the battery capacity (Wh) is marked.

✔ If the Wh rating is not available, both the battery capacity (Ah) and voltage (V) must be marked.

✔ Unmarked batteries with no Wh, Ah, or V information require airline approval.

✔ Damaged or deformed batteries require airline approval.

⭐ Capacity Guidelines

🟢
Up to 100 Wh Allowed in carry-on baggage
🟡
Over 100 Wh up to 160 Wh Allowed in carry-on baggage with airline approval
🔴
Over 160 Wh Not allowed in carry-on baggage

📌 Please Note


Batteries over 100 Wh, unmarked batteries, and damaged or deformed batteries can be checked at your airline's check-in counter before departure.

For the most accurate information, please contact your airline.
