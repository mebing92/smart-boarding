💇 Cordless Hair Iron

How should I pack a cordless hair iron?

Cordless hair irons are generally not allowed in either carry-on baggage or checked baggage.

However, some cordless hair irons may be allowed in carry-on baggage with airline approval if they meet the conditions below.

📌 Check the Type

🔋 Removable Battery

Remove the battery first.

May be carried in carry-on baggage with airline approval.

※ The removed battery is subject to the lithium battery rules.

✈️ Airplane Mode Supported

Enable Airplane Mode first.

May be carried in carry-on baggage with airline approval.

※ Applies only to products that support Airplane Mode.

❌ Standard Model

Cordless hair irons without a removable battery or Airplane Mode support

are not allowed in either carry-on baggage or checked baggage.

⭐ Remember

✔ Airline approval is required even if the product has a removable battery or Airplane Mode.

✔ Approval requirements may vary depending on your airline.

📌 Please Note

Transportation rules and approval requirements may vary depending on your airline.

For the most accurate information, please contact your airline.
