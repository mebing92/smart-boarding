🧳 Checked Baggage

Common items that can be transported as checked baggage.

📌 Check First

Items that cannot be carried in your carry-on baggage can instead be transported as checked baggage.

Check some common examples below.

🔪 Knives · Multi-tools · Tools

Knives, multi-tools, scissors, and tools
should be transported as checked baggage.

🧸 Toy Guns · Toy Knives

Toy guns and toy knives that closely resemble real weapons
should be transported as checked baggage.

🛡️ Self-defense Items

Knuckle dusters, handcuffs, batons, stun guns, and pepper spray
should be transported as checked baggage.

🧴 Liquids Over 100 mL

Shampoo, conditioner, kimchi, beverages, and more
Liquids over 100 mL should be transported as checked baggage.

⛺ Tent Pegs · Camping Equipment

Tents, tent pegs, lanterns, and camping equipment
should be transported as checked baggage.

⚠️ Most tent pegs are longer than 6 cm and may not be allowed in carry-on baggage.

🚲 Bicycle Tools · Sports Equipment

Bicycle multi-tools, hex keys, and sports equipment
should be transported as checked baggage.

📌 Please Note

Lithium batteries, e-cigarettes, and lighters cannot be transported as checked baggage.
