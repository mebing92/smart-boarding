✈️ Carry-on Baggage

What can I bring in my carry-on baggage?

💧 Liquids

Liquids in containers of 100 mL or less are allowed in your carry-on baggage.

Up to 10 containers per passenger, with a total volume of up to 1 L.

Learn More

💻 Electronics

Laptops, tablets, cameras,
hair dryers, plug-in hair irons, and more.

Most electronic devices are allowed in your carry-on baggage.

🚬 E-cigarettes & Lighters

E-cigarettes are allowed in your carry-on baggage.

One lighter per passenger is allowed.
However, plasma electric lighters are prohibited.

🔋 Lithium Batteries

Lithium batteries are allowed in your carry-on baggage.

Restrictions may vary depending on the battery capacity and quantity.

Learn More

📌 Please Note

Baggage regulations may vary depending on your airline's policy.
