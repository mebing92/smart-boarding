🗑 Voluntary Disposal

Please read the information below.

💡 Please Read First


Items that have been disposed of cannot be returned.

Before disposing of your item, please check whether baggage storage or checked baggage is available.

👮 Disposal at Security Screening

Items prohibited during Security Screening will be collected by a Security Officer.

Please ask a Security Officer for assistance.

♻️ Disposal Process

Disposed items are processed through lawful procedures, including donation, in accordance with applicable regulations.
