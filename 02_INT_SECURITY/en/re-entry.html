🔄 Re-entering Security Screening

If you have completed the required procedure, please follow the steps below to re-enter Security Screening.

① Wear Your Re-entry Pass

Before leaving the restricted area, please wear the Re-entry Pass provided by the staff and return to the Departure Area.

② Follow the Priority Screening Line

After completing ID verification, please follow the pink Priority Screening Line.

③ Use the Re-entry Entrance

Please enter through the Staff & Re-entry Passenger Entrance .

④ Follow the Directional Arrows

After passing through the entrance, follow the arrows to the Priority Screening Area.

⑤ Wait at the Priority Screening Area

Please wait in the Priority Screening Area.

A Security Officer will guide you through Security Screening.

💡 Please Note

• Please wear your Re-entry Pass until Security Screening is complete.

• Please follow the Priority Screening Line.

• Please follow the instructions provided by the Security Officer.
