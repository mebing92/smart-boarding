💧 Liquids

How should you carry liquids?

Basic Rule

Only liquids in containers of 100 mL or less are allowed in carry-on baggage.

Place them in one 1 L transparent resealable plastic bag.

📌 Please Check

Foods containing even a small amount of liquid are also subject to the 100 mL liquid rule.

Lip balm, hair wax, cleansing balm, toner pads, cleansing wipes, and wet wipes are also subject to the 100 mL liquid rule.

Please empty all liquids from your tumbler.

Drinking beverages, such as water and coffee, must be finished or discarded before Security Screening.

⭐ Remember

✔ Liquid restrictions are based on the container capacity, not the amount of liquid inside.

✔ Containers without a capacity label may not be allowed in carry-on baggage.

📌 Please Note

For specific restrictions, please contact your airline.
