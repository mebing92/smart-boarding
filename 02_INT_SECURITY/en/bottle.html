SMART BOARDING

International Security Screening Guide ✈️

🧴 Buy Empty Bottles

Liquids over 100 mL may be carried on board after being transferred into containers of 100 mL or less.

📌 Before You Begin

Liquids transferred into containers of 100 mL or less are allowed in carry-on baggage.

Please check the locations where empty bottles are available.

⭐ Recommended Store

We recommend Lucky Box Gift Shop on the same floor as the Departure Area.

It provides the most convenient access to Security Screening.

⭐ Lucky Box Gift Shop

Located on the 2nd Floor of the International Terminal.

🕖 Hours 07:00 ~ 21:00

After purchasing an empty bottle, you can proceed directly to Security Screening.

🏪 emart24 Convenience Store

Located on the 1st Floor of the International Terminal.

🕔 Hours 05:00 ~ 22:00

Convenient if you plan to visit the convenience store before departure.

💡 How to Use

① Buy an empty bottle
↓
② Transfer the liquid into a container of 100 mL or less
↓
③ Place it in a 1 L transparent resealable plastic bag
↓
④ Go through Security Screening

📌 Please Note

Liquid restrictions are based on the container capacity (100 mL) rather than the amount of liquid inside.

Containers without a capacity label may not be allowed in carry-on baggage.

For more information about liquid restrictions, please select "Learn More."
