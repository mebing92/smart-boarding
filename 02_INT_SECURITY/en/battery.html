🔋 Power Banks

How should you carry power banks?

Power banks are allowed only in carry-on baggage.

They are not allowed in checked baggage.

📌 Quantity Limits

🔋 Portable Power Banks

Up to 2 per passenger.

※ For batteries over 100 Wh, please check the capacity limits below.

📷 Spare Batteries for Electronic Devices

Up to 5 per passenger.

💻 Electronic Devices with Lithium Batteries

Airline approval is required for 15 or more devices.

💡 Please Check


✔ Please check whether the battery capacity (Wh) is marked.

✔ If the Wh rating is not marked, both capacity (Ah) and voltage (V) must be indicated.

✔ Batteries without Wh, Ah, or V markings require airline approval.

✔ Damaged or deformed batteries also require airline approval.

⭐ Battery Capacity Limits

🟢
100 Wh or less Allowed in carry-on baggage
🟡
Over 100 Wh up to 160 Wh Allowed in carry-on baggage with airline approval
🔴
Over 160 Wh Not allowed in carry-on baggage

📌 Please Note


Batteries over 100 Wh, unmarked batteries, and damaged or deformed batteries can be checked in advance at the Airline Counter.

For more information, please contact your airline.
