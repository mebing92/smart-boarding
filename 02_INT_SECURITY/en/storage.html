📦 Baggage Storage

If your departure time is approaching, baggage storage may not be available.

💡 TIP

Please make sure you have enough time.

The baggage storage facility is located in the Domestic Terminal.

A round trip may take about 20 minutes. Therefore, we recommend using the storage service at least 1 hour before departure.

📍 Storage Information

Located next to GATE 2 on the 1st Floor of the Domestic Terminal.

• Storage service is available for a fee.
• Hours : 05:00 ~ 20:00
 • Storage fees vary according to the storage facility.
• Please check detailed information at the storage counter.

💳 Storage Fees

Storage fees may vary depending on the size of your item and the storage period.

🚶 How to Use Baggage Storage

Please ask a Security Officer for assistance.

① Go to the baggage storage facility on the 1st Floor of the Domestic Terminal
↓
② Check the fee based on the item size and storage period, then store your item
↓
③ Re-enter the Departure Area
↓
④ Go through Security Screening again
